MaskedVerbalizer: Automatic Verbalizer Construction for Few-Shot Text Classification in Low-Resource Right-to-Left Languages

Faizan Ali · Furqan Sikandar · Areeba Waqar · Faizad Ullah · Mubashar Mushtaq

We developed an automated verbalizer construction technique for few-shot text classification in low-resource right-to-left languages. The method uses a model's own masked-token predictions to automatically identify class-specific label words from only four examples per class.

Challenge

Low-resource languages such as Urdu, Arabic, Pashto, and Sindhi have limited labeled data, making few-shot text classification challenging.

Approach

We developed MASKEDVERBALIZER, an automated verbalizer construction technique that uses a model's own masked-token predictions to identify class-specific label words from only 4 examples per class. The approach was evaluated with mBERT, XLM-RoBERTa, and LLaMA 3.2 (1B) across 5 languages and 6 datasets.

Results

XLM-RoBERTa achieved up to 92.72% accuracy, with 89.77% on Urdu News and 84.28% on Sindhi, demonstrating substantial improvements over existing few-shot verbalizer methods.
